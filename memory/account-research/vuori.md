# Account Research — Vuori (ACC005)

**Industry:** Retail & eCommerce (DTC performance apparel) · **HQ:** Carlsbad, CA · **Size:** ~1,000+

## Overview
Vuori is a fast-growing direct-to-consumer athletic and lifestyle apparel brand (founded 2015). It
sells through its own ecommerce storefront and a rapidly expanding retail footprint, and has raised
large growth rounds (incl. a major SoftBank-led investment), putting it firmly in the hyper-growth
DTC midmarket. The business is brand- and retention-led with a heavy lifecycle-marketing motion.

## Why now (signals)
- Hyper-growth DTC with rich, high-volume first-party customer + order data.
- Aggressive retail expansion → omnichannel identity and audience needs (online + store).
- Retention/LTV and paid-social efficiency are board-level priorities at this scale.

## Data stack (inferred ICP fit)
Likely a cloud warehouse (Snowflake) as the system of record, with **Klaviyo** for lifecycle email/SMS
and **Meta/Google** for paid social. *(Inferred — to be confirmed in discovery.)*

## Northwind use case
Warehouse-native **audience activation**: build retention, win-back, and lookalike audiences directly
on the warehouse and sync them to Klaviyo + Meta without the lifecycle team rebuilding lists by hand
or waiting on data engineering. Strong fit for **AI Decisioning** on next-best-offer once audiences
are flowing.

## Personas
- **Economic buyer:** VP Growth / VP Marketing.
- **Champion:** Director of Lifecycle / Retention Marketing.
- **Technical:** Analytics Engineer / Data Platform lead.

## Risks / notes
Brand-led org may under-invest in data tooling vs. creative; lead with conversion/LTV proof, not
infrastructure. Confirm warehouse + ESP in discovery.
