# Account Research — Brooklinen (ACC008)

**Industry:** Retail & eCommerce (DTC home goods / bedding) · **HQ:** Brooklyn, NY · **Size:** ~50–200

## Overview
Brooklinen is a profitable, retention-driven direct-to-consumer home-goods brand (founded 2014),
one of the original DTC bedding players. Lean team, strong repeat-purchase and lifecycle motion, with
a growing retail/wholesale presence.

## Why now (signals)
- Mature DTC with deep first-party purchase history → high-value retention + replenishment audiences.
- Lean marketing team is stretched; manual audience building in the ESP is a real bottleneck.
- Margin-focused (profitable DTC) → strong appetite for efficiency and suppression to cut wasted spend.

## Data stack (inferred ICP fit)
Likely a cloud warehouse (**Snowflake**) with **Klaviyo** for lifecycle and the major ad platforms for
acquisition/retargeting. *(Inferred — confirm in discovery.)*

## Northwind use case
Warehouse-native **lifecycle + suppression audiences**: replenishment, win-back, VIP, and
high-AOV segments synced to Klaviyo + ads, plus suppression of recent purchasers/unsubscribes to cut
wasted spend — without the lean team hand-building lists each campaign.

## Personas
- **Economic buyer:** Director of Ecommerce / Head of Marketing.
- **Champion:** Head of Retention / Lifecycle Marketing.
- **Technical:** Data/Analytics (often a single owner or agency).

## Risks / notes
Small team = limited technical bandwidth; lead with managed, low-lift activation and fast ROI.
Confirm whether data lives in a true warehouse vs. only in the ESP.
