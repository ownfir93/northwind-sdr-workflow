# Account Research — Calendly (ACC007)

**Industry:** Software / SaaS (scheduling, PLG) · **HQ:** Atlanta, GA · **Size:** ~200–500

## Overview
Calendly is a product-led scheduling SaaS with a very large freemium user base, founded 2013 and
valued around $3B after its 2021 raise. Classic PLG motion: huge top-of-funnel, monetized via
self-serve upgrades and a sales-assist motion for teams/enterprise.

## Why now (signals)
- Enormous product-usage signal volume but a generic, one-size nurture — activation milestones aren't
  reaching marketing in real time.
- PLG orgs at this scale need product data → marketing/sales tools to drive expansion and conversion.
- Demand-gen + lifecycle teams want to trigger on "aha-moment" usage, not just form fills.

## Data stack (inferred ICP fit)
Likely a cloud warehouse (**BigQuery**) with product events, and a marketing automation platform
(**Marketo** or similar). *(Inferred — confirm in discovery.)*

## Northwind use case
**PLG activation**: sync product-usage milestones (created first event, invited a teammate, hit a
usage threshold) from the warehouse to the MAP and ads to drive timely nurture, PQL routing to sales,
and expansion plays — instead of product signals sitting trapped in the warehouse.

## Personas
- **Economic buyer:** VP Marketing / Director of Demand Gen.
- **Champion:** Demand Gen / Growth PM.
- **Technical:** Analytics Engineering.

## Risks / notes
PLG self-serve culture means a light, fast eval; lead with a single high-value activation use case and
a same-week pilot. Multiple stakeholders (growth + product + data).
